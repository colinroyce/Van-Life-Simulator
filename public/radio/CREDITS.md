# Radio music credits

Every track here is in the public domain or released under CC0, so no attribution is legally
required. Credit is given anyway. The files were re-encoded to AAC (`.m4a`) for size:
96 kbps stereo, and 64 kbps mono for the 78 rpm recordings.

US sound recordings published in 1925 or earlier are in the public domain (Music Modernization
Act). The road trip classics are all 1918–1925 recordings.
